About Me

I'm a B.Com (Hons) student at Jagran College of Arts, Science & Commerce with a deep interest in understanding how data shapes business strategy and financial performance. Over time, my curiosity for numbers has evolved into a strong passion for Business Analytics, where insights meet impact.

Alongside my graduation, I developed a deeper interest in how data supports business decisions. This led me to complete the Data Analytics program at ICT Academy, IIT Kanpur , where I worked on data handling, analytical reasoning, and transforming raw information into meaningful insights.

After completing my semester examinations, I further strengthened my technical foundation by completing the CCC certification, enhancing my understanding of computer fundamentals and practical digital tools.

My professional exposure across accounting, financial services, and customer relationship management has shaped my analytical thinking, communication skills, and ability to work effectively in fast-paced environments.

I enjoy working with data, simplifying complexity, and contributing to decisions that create real business impact. My goal is to build a career where analytics, finance, and strategy intersect to drive smarter, insight-driven decisions.
